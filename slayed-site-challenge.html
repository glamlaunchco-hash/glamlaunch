    Which package should I pick?

    Start with $297 if you need a professional one-page site. Choose $497 to add a freebie or lead magnet that grows your list. Choose $797 for four pages plus the email and follow-up foundation that keeps people coming back.

    Are email and booking tools included?

    GlamLaunch sets up and connects them. The software itself, such as your email platform, booking app, domain and hosting, is paid to those providers unless we confirm otherwise with you.

    Can I nominate another business?

    Yes. Use the claim form, add the business in the notes and tell us why it deserves a glow-up.
